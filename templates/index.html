📌 How It Works

A simple, transparent process for managing your final year projects

1️⃣
Student Registration & Topic Selection

Register using your matric number, then browse available topics and submit your chosen project topic.

2️⃣
Supervisor Clearance

Your assigned supervisor reviews your topic and clears you for presentation when satisfied.

3️⃣
Payment & Presentation

After clearance, make the presentation fee. You become eligible to present to the panel.
